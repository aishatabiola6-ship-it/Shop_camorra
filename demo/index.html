The customer journey

From first swipe to front door

1

Explore & swipe

An 18+ welcome, then swipe through hot picks, browse by mood and save favourites with a ♡.

2

Sign in your way

Google, Apple or email. A 6-digit code arrives by email, and customers can create a password for next time.

3

Learn how it works

Every product page explains how to use it, step by step – then pick a colour or size and add to bag.

4

Pay securely

Card, bank transfer or USSD through Flutterwave. The payment is double-checked with Flutterwave automatically.

5

Add delivery details

Name, WhatsApp number and address. The order is saved to the shop's Google Sheet instantly.

6

Delivery on WhatsApp

The customer gets an order confirmation email, and the delivery date and fee are sent on WhatsApp.
