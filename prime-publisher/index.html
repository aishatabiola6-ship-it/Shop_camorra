With more than five years as a head teacher, following years as an assistant head teacher and classroom teacher, she brings first-hand knowledge of children’s learning needs to Prime Publisher.

That experience guides our commitment to accurate, up-to-date past questions and thoughtful books that give young learners a strong foundation.
