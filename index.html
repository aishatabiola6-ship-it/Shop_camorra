Questions

Good to know

How do I pay?

Pay by card or bank transfer at checkout. In Lagos you can also pay the rider on delivery. Prefer to chat? Send your order on WhatsApp and we'll sort it there.

How much is delivery?

We deliver to all 36 states and FCT. The fee depends on your state and is shown at checkout before you pay:

What if my size doesn't fit?

Message us within 48 hours of delivery. Unworn pairs in their original box can be exchanged for another size, subject to stock.

How do I find my size?

Measure your foot from heel to longest toe in cm and send it to us. As a guide: 23.5cm ≈ EU 37, 24cm ≈ EU 38, 24.5cm ≈ EU 39, 25cm ≈ EU 40.
