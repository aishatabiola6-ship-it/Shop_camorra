There is a line of yours I still carry with me: “The period we shared this year was the happiest I’ve ever had this year.” I cannot say whether you have known a happier moment since, but those words stayed. They were only words, and yet it has always been the smallest things that matter most to me.

From the twenty-fourth of April to the twenty-first of July, we spoke without pause. No one before you had ever counted down the days to see me. The first time we met, you made me feel whole again, and you gave me a gentler way of seeing something I had long misunderstood.

It may surprise you that I still feel this way. I do. But I have not come to press my feelings upon you; I have come to thank you. Because of you, I began to take serious things seriously. I found worth in things I once ignored. I did more, and the more is mine, wholly and only mine.

You said things to me that were unfair, and I never once had an unkind word to give in return. Let me simply say this: I understand that you came to see me differently. I still see you the same. I truly changed. I only changed too late.

I had pictured us reading side by side, you preparing for your next MB, and I for my end-of-semester exams. That picture has been taken from me, and I am still learning how to look away from it.

I want to stop feeling this way, but the person I am right now does not know what to do with all of this. I want to leave you in peace. I do not want to cheer for you from the quiet distance of friendship. I do not want anything at all. I only want to wake two weeks from now and feel well again.

Perhaps our paths will cross again someday. Until then, I do not want to go to the clinic, read, sit through my online classes and fall into bed each night still thinking of how much I love you, knowing there is nothing I can do with that love.

I wish this were not happening to me. I wish I could be the calm, untouchable one whom nothing ever reaches.

But I am not. And so I have written it down.

Ige Adubi
